## Description (Apple 4,000 / Google 4,000)
CardFlip tells you what your trading cards are worth.

Point your phone at a card and CardFlip reads it: the name, the set, the number and the printing. You see its price
right away, along with how it has moved over the last month.

Five games: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Disney Lorcana and One Piece.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Scan a card with your camera and see its price
• Search any card by name or number, free
• Keep your cards in Inventory and watch your collection's total value
• Add cards to your Watchlist and get an alert when a price moves
• See price history charts for every card
• List a card on eBay from the app, with the title, photos and price filled in
• Track what sold, what you paid and what you earned

PRICES
Prices come from recent market data and are checked against real sales. When a price looks off, CardFlip says so
instead of guessing.
