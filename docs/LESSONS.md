## 10-18 · eBay price checks: reprints and look-alike printings hide right prices (found working /admin/prices by hand)
- What: of ~60 flagged cards checked against eBay sold, ~20 had a really wrong price (fixed: new Lorcana Hyperia City, prices frozen for weeks, Retro/Premium Pack reprints with $100-680 junk numbers, $213k junk Yu-Gi-Oh) but most flags were FALSE: the eBay asks the rule compares against were (a) reprints: the 2026 "30th Celebration" + 2021 Celebrations copies of vintage holos ($5-15 next to $100-500 originals, often listed without saying "reprint"), (b) cheaper look-alike printings: Magic textured / Japan showcase / fracture foils read against the plain card, Yu-Gi-Oh North American vs European LOB prints, theme-deck non-holos, (c) fakes ("Collectable Pokemon...", "Fan Art").
- Fix: matcher rejects 30th/Celebration titles (unless the card is from that set), "Collectable"/"Fan Art", and needs the treatment word or the card's own number for Magic treatment foils (commit only). Hidden-but-right cards got "keep"; wrong ones got checked prices.
- Lesson: a big gap to the asks is NOT proof the price is wrong for vintage cards; unmarked reprints sit at the bottom of every vintage search now. Read the sold list, split originals from reprints, and use the originals.

## 10-19 Vercel bill: three crawlers were 91% of a day's requests
- Chris: "why is this so crazy today" (ISR Writes $1.67 vs ~$0.40). Vercel Observability > ISR showed /cards/[game]/[set]/[card] with 104K writes on 61K unique paths in 12 h and only 16K reads; AI Traffic + Firewall Traffic named them: meta-webindexer 65K, AionBot 31K, Amzn-SearchBot 18K of 125K requests.
- Cause: card pages are rendered on demand (revalidate 2 days, generateStaticParams empty), so a crawler walking the catalog pays an ISR write per page. robots.txt blocked the 10-05 list but not these three names.
- Fix: robots.ts adds the three; Vercel Firewall deny rule (Chris clicked Deny, classifier blocked me) for meta-webindexer, widen to the other two.
- Lesson: when the Vercel chart jumps, read Observability > ISR (writes by route) and Firewall > Traffic (top user agents) first; the runtime-logs API only streams live seconds. Build CPU (Oct 4-8) and Blob ops (Oct 12-13, picture uploads) explained the earlier spikes; never the Claude model.
