# Phone checks for Chris (written 10-18)

One sitting, about 20 minutes. Use your phone, signed in to CardFlip, on cardflip.io.
After each part, tell me "part N ok" or what looked wrong. Skip any part you can't do; I'll keep it on the list.

## Part 1. Scanner (have 2-3 real cards nearby)
1. Open cardflip.io/app and tap **Scan**.
   - You should see corner brackets and a short hint line. In a dark room the hint turns amber and says it's too dark.
2. Tap the **"..."** button on the scanner.
   - You should see a sheet with Condition, Light, Tips, Share and Help. Nothing cut off at the edges.
3. Pick a condition (for example LP), close the sheet, open it again.
   - You should see LP still picked.
4. Scan any Pokémon card.
   - You should see the card within about 3 seconds, the price, "List it at", and "vs N listings".
5. If you own one: scan a **Prismatic Evolutions Poké Ball or Master Ball** reverse, or an **Ascended Heroes** ball / Energy Symbol reverse.
   - You should see the Printing already set to that pattern (not plain Reverse Holo) with its own price.
6. If you own one: scan a **Vivillon 17/146** (XY base set).
   - Orange wings: "High Plains" (~$9). Pink wings: plain Vivillon (~$2).
7. On a scanned card, tap **"Not this card?"** (or the card name).
   - You should see big pictures in a grid, names wrap. Tap another one: the result swaps. Swipe down closes it.
8. On a scan row, tap the **Clear** pill.
   - You should see "Cleared" and the row gone. Your Inventory keeps the card if you'd saved it.
9. Tap **Done** (top right).
   - "Done" sits on one line, no wrap.

## Part 2. Card pages
10. Open cardflip.io/cards/pokemon/base-set/charizard--base1-4
    - From ~10-20 you should see about **$600** and "Checked against recent eBay sales". (Before then it may still say "looks off"; that's the 2-day page cache.)
11. On any card page, tap **"Price looks wrong?"**, then **Too high**.
    - You should see a thank-you line. Tap again: it stays at one vote.
12. Scroll down on the same page.
    - You should see "Buy on eBay" and the small commission line under it.
13. Open cardflip.io/cards/magic and scroll the Sets list.
    - Nothing scrolls sideways. Long set names end in "…".

## Part 3. Watchlist and Search
14. Open **Search** in the app before typing anything.
    - You should see the picture grid and "My Watchlist" for the game picked at the top.
15. Switch the game pill to a game you have nothing watched in.
    - You should see a short "Tap ☆" line, not an empty box.
16. Open **Watchlist**.
    - The game pill shows a count, like "Pokémon · 6". Each tile shows price and ▲/▼ % on ONE line.
17. Pick a game with no watched cards.
    - You should see "No Magic cards on your watchlist" and a "Show Pokémon (6)" button.

## Part 4. Inventory
18. Open **Inventory**, tap a card, tap the cost box.
    - You should see the number keypad with a "." key.
19. Close it. On the value chart, tap **All**.
    - The chart shows your whole history.
20. Tap a card, look for **"Full price page →"** and tap it.
    - It opens that card's public price page.

## Part 5. Sign-in (do this one signed OUT, in a private tab)
21. Open cardflip.io/login, type a made-up email and any password, tap Log In.
    - You should see "Incorrect email or password. Signed up with Google or X? ..."
22. Log in for real with Google.
    - You land signed in. The top menu shows "Log out" and "Open the App" with no flash of "Log In".
23. If Google's "Continue as ..." pop-up shows on a public page, close it with the X, then reload.
    - It never comes back on that phone.

## Part 6. Admin (once, on the phone)
24. Open cardflip.io/admin and sign in (password + emailed code).
    - You land on Overview first try, no bounce back to the sign-in page.
25. Tap **Sign Out**, then open cardflip.io/admin again.
    - You should see the sign-in page (the old session is really over).

## When they happen (no need to set up)
- Your next eBay listing: it goes up as quantity 1.
- First card you list for $20-100 with envelope shipping: eBay accepts the "CardFlip letter" postage.
- A real sale over $20: it shows in Earned with the postage you picked.
