# Three phone checks (10-22). Your phone, signed in to cardflip.io. About 5 minutes.

Tell me "all three fine" or which number looked wrong. Nothing here costs a scan except check 2 (one scan).

## 1. Number keypad on the cost box
1. Open the app, tap **Inventory** at the bottom.
2. Tap any card to open it.
3. Tap the **What did you pay?** box.
   You should see: the phone's NUMBER keypad with a "." key, not the letter keyboard.
4. Tap **Mark as Sold**, then tap the sold-price box.
   You should see: the same number keypad.
5. Tap **Cancel** so nothing is marked sold.

## 2. Scan chip + the price reveal (one scan)
1. Tap **Scan** at the bottom and point the camera at any card you own.
2. Hold still; wait for the read.
   You should see, in this order:
   - A chip under the camera: the card's art on the left, "MATCH FOUND", the card's name and set, and a **Clear** button at the top right beside the name.
   - A GREEN band under the name with the price counting up and then popping ONCE when it lands. The price always shows cents ($28.00, never $28).
   - To the right of the price: a condition pill ("Near Mint"). If the printing name is long it is cut short inside the pill; the price never moves.
3. Now scan a card while covering its number with your thumb (any card that comes in several printings, a Charizard is ideal).
   You should see: "PRICING..." in the green band for a few seconds, then ONE price. No big number that then changes.
4. If that card is worth $100 or more, you should also see the "Nice pull!" moment once, after the price settles, not before.

## 3. Mark as Sold waits for Verify Match
1. Tap **Inventory**, switch to the list view (the list icon at the top).
2. Find a card that still shows **Verify Match**.
   You should see: NO "Mark as Sold" on that row. Tap the row: the popup offers "Yes, This Is My Card" and Delete, nothing about selling.
3. Tap **Yes, This Is My Card**.
   You should see: "Mark as Sold" appears for that card.
4. A sealed product (a booster box or pack, if you have one) shows Mark as Sold straight away, no verify step.

Done. If any step shows something else, tell me the number and what you saw.
