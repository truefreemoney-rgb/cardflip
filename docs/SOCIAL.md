# Social media: set-up and the first month

Written 2026-09-10 for board task #26 ("I want to develop social media
presence. Please give me step by step instructions." Chris: Instagram, X,
TikTok). Everything below is for Chris, on a phone, in order. Total time for
Part 1 and 2 is about an hour.

Rules that apply here as everywhere on CardFlip: real cards, real prices,
real screens (DESIGN.md "Data honesty"). Voice stays plain and dry, no
exclamation marks, no hype words. Buttons and handles in the app are Title
Case; captions are sentence case.

## Part 1 — decide three things before you open any app (10 min)

1. **One handle for all three.** People search the same name everywhere.
   Try these in order and stop at the first one free on all three:
   `cardflipio` → `cardflipapp` → `getcardflip`.
   (`cardflip` alone is almost certainly taken. X allows only letters,
   numbers and `_`, max 15 characters, so no dots. `cardflipio` is 10.)
   Check X at `x.com/<handle>`, Instagram at `instagram.com/<handle>`,
   TikTok at `tiktok.com/@<handle>`. A "page not found" means it is free.
2. **One email and one phone.** Use `support@cardflip.io` for all three
   (it is the business mailbox already). Your phone number for the codes.
   Never a personal Gmail; if you ever hand social to someone else, the
   accounts stay with the business.
3. **The picture and the words.** Same on all three.
   - Profile picture: the CF card mark. Open
     `https://cardflip.io/brand/cardflip-icon.png` on the phone, long-press,
     Save Image. It is 512×512, which every platform accepts, and it stays
     readable inside the round crop.
   - Display name: `CardFlip`
   - Website: `https://cardflip.io`
   - Bio (Instagram and X, up to 150 characters):
     `Scan a Pokémon card. Get the real price. List it on eBay. 10 free
     scans at cardflip.io`
     (No "Magic coming" line: the site only claims what it does today, and
     Magic is still admin-only.)
   - Bio (TikTok, 80 characters max):
     `Scan a card. Get the real price. List it on eBay. 10 free scans below`
   - Category, when asked: Software / App.

## Part 2 — make the accounts (about 15 min each)

Do them in this order. Turn on two-step on each one before you leave it;
a lost social account is a bad week.

### Instagram

1. Instagram app → Create new account → sign up with **email**
   (`support@cardflip.io`) → the code arrives in Fastmail.
2. Full name `CardFlip`, username = the handle from Part 1, a strong
   password saved in the phone's password manager.
3. Skip "find friends" and "follow suggestions" for now.
4. Profile → Edit profile → add the picture, the bio, the website link.
5. Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account →
   Business → category Software. This unlocks the link in bio, insights,
   and the "Contact" button. Skip Facebook linking if it asks.
6. Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security → Two-factor
   authentication → Authentication app (same app you use for CardFlip's own
   two-step). Save the backup codes in the password manager.

### X

1. `x.com/i/flow/signup` → Create account → name `CardFlip` → use email
   instead of phone → `support@cardflip.io` → code from Fastmail.
2. X assigns a random username first. Settings and privacy → Your account
   → Account information → Username → change it to the handle.
3. Profile → Edit profile: picture (400×400 is what X wants; the 512 mark
   is fine), bio, website `cardflip.io`, location `United States`.
4. Header image (1500×500): optional today. A photo of your own cards on a
   dark table works, or leave it plain until there is time. Do not use a
   picture you did not take.
5. Settings → Security and account access → Security → Two-factor
   authentication → Authentication app.
6. Settings → Privacy and safety → Direct messages: allow message requests
   from everyone. That is where "can you price this?" arrives.

### TikTok

1. TikTok app → Sign up → Use phone or email → Email → `support@cardflip.io`
   → code from Fastmail. Birthday must be the business owner's real one.
2. Username: the handle. Name: `CardFlip`.
3. Profile → Edit profile: picture, name, bio (the short one).
4. Settings and privacy → Account → Switch to Business Account → category
   Software. **The website link in the bio only shows on a Business
   account**, which is the whole point of TikTok for us.
5. Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → 2-step verification →
   Authenticator app.

When all three exist, **reply on the board with the three handles.** The
runner will put them in the site footer and in the site's organisation
data so Google links the profiles to cardflip.io.

## Part 3 — the first three posts (one weekend)

One video, posted to all three. Vertical, 15 to 30 seconds, phone screen
recording, no talking needed. Same video on Instagram (Reel), TikTok, and X.

**How to record.** Open CardFlip from the home-screen icon (full screen,
no browser bar). Swipe down for Control Center → Screen Recording (add it
under Settings → Control Center if it is missing). Keep the scanner's
sound toggle on so the chime is in the recording. Stop, then trim the
start and end in Photos.

1. **Post 1: one scan, start to finish.** A card you own with a real price
   worth seeing (a $100+ card, so the reveal does the "Nice pull" stamp).
   Point the camera, capture, let the price count up, stop recording.
   Caption: `Point the phone at a card. This is what happens. 10 free
   scans at cardflip.io`
2. **Post 2: three cards, three prices.** Scan three cards back to back.
   Caption: `What are these worth today. Scanned, priced, done.`
3. **Post 3: card to eBay listing.** Scan, tap Check and publish, show the
   listing come up on eBay. Caption: `From the binder to an eBay listing
   in about a minute.`

Hashtags, five at most, the same five every time:
`#pokemontcg #pokemoncards #tcg #cardcollector #ebayseller`
(On X, hashtags do little; use one or none.)

## Part 4 — the weekly rhythm (20 minutes a day)

- **Three posts a week**, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, the same video on
  all three. Record two or three on Sunday so the week is done.
- **The format that repeats:** "What's this worth?" A follower sends a
  photo or names a card; you scan yours or look it up in Search Cards
  and post the price. It is the product doing its own advertising.
- **Reply to every comment the same day.** One line, plain, no arguing.
  A wrong price in the comments is a bug report: paste it on the board.
- **Follow 20 accounts a week** in the Pokémon-selling corner (eBay
  sellers, local shops, pack openers), and comment on two of their posts
  a day with something true. That is where the first 500 followers come
  from, not from your own posts.
- **Once a month** look at the numbers each app shows (Instagram Insights,
  TikTok Analytics, X Analytics): which video got the most watch time.
  Make more of that one.

**Do not:** buy followers, post a price you did not scan, use another
person's card photo, post the same clip twice in a week, or reply to
trolls.

## One thing to know before you push it

`docs/SOFT-LAUNCH.md` (09-06) says not to post publicly until the invite
cohort's numbers are read, because a public "try it free" brings lookers
who burn the eBay Browse quota (5,000 a day). Setting up the accounts and
posting the scan videos is fine now; hold the "10 free scans" line in the
captions until the cohort is done, or say on the board that the rule is
dropped and the captions above stand as written.
